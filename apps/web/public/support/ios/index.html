Lupi support

For help with Lupi on iPhone or iPad, email alex@lupinesci.com.

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, your device model and iOS version. Lupi Settings has Copy build diagnostics if you would like to include the app’s version and source revision. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes or payment-card details.

Camera and room tracking

Lupi opens the camera only when you choose Play. If permission was refused, open iOS Settings and allow camera access for Lupi. In Play, move the device slowly so ARKit can find surfaces. Room tracking requires a supported iPhone or iPad; the Simulator cannot test it.

If tracking is unavailable, return Home to explore molecules and your collection. You can retry room tracking from the recovery controls. LiDAR is optional; devices without it use detected planes.

Saved trophies and shelves

The initial App Store release stores your collection on this device and does not offer account sign-in or cloud sync. If a saved room cannot be matched, use the shelf recovery options rather than erasing your collection.

Erase this device’s collection permanently removes its local trophies, rooms and shelves. Contact support before using it if you are trying to recover missing work.

Finding a molecule

Exact bundled names and formulas, such as caffeine or H2O, work offline. Description matching uses an online service and may be unavailable. It suggests an existing bundled structure; it does not generate molecular coordinates. Read how online descriptions are handled.

Physical merchandise orders

Lupi is free to use. Physical merchandise is sold through the separate Shopify shop when a verified offer is available, with shipping to supported US addresses. For an order question, email alex@lupinesci.com and include the order number and the issue. Do not include card details. Buying merchandise does not unlock app features.

Privacy questions

For privacy or data requests, use the same contact address and tell us which feature or message your request concerns. The native app privacy policy explains local room data, online search and support messages.
