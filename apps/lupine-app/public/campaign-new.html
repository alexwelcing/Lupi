Anchor policy — frozen preset

The protocol pays DFT only for path endpoints, each model's predicted minimum, and a ±1 window around its predicted maximum (±2 on short paths); budget ≤ 7 anchors/path. Anchors are shared across models (union) — that sharing is the savings stack.

Acceptance & budget

Verdict: win at barrier MAE ≤ 40 meV, strong win ≤ 15 meV (preregistered thresholds).

Runs consume subscription compute. Launch is limited to the reviewed panel and registered model set; missing or contradictory evidence is refused.
