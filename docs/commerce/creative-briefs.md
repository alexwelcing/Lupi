# Three molecule campaign briefs

These are copy-ready drafts for the [staged dual launch](dual-launch.md), not published ads or artwork approvals. Use the matching exact-version Lupi assets. Copy changes do not recolor, replace or approve molecular imagery; changed crops, compositions and final ad assets need their own applicable review. Existing artwork/ad approvals remain evidence for those exact artifacts only.

Each story has two end frames: an exploration CTA for the free app once publicly available, and a purchase CTA only for an accepted physical offer. Before those gates, use the working molecule exploration route and honest availability status. Do not advertise a purchase that the destination cannot complete. Reddit requires the advertised offer on the landing page, matching destination/display URLs and functioning navigation. [Reddit editorial requirements](https://business.reddithelp.com/articles/Knowledge/Reddit-Advertising-Policy-Editorial-Requirements)

## Caffeine — your first cup

- **Campaign:** `us26-caffeine-01`; native molecule `caffeine`; landing `/launch/caffeine/`.
- **Headline:** Your first cup. A closer look.
- **Body:** A familiar ritual, seen molecule by molecule. Explore caffeine in Lupi. Physical merchandise is optional.
- **Gift thought:** Recognize the coffee ritual of someone you know, without choosing their coffee equipment for them.
- **Product priority:** Quiet Capsule Black Comfort Colors 1717 tee, then Charcoal/White Richardson 256 Umpqua cap; both remain readiness candidates. Do not substitute the historical Shopify caffeine print bindings.
- **15-second edit:** 0–3 seconds: the familiar first-cup gesture. 3–8: orbit the verified 24-atom caffeine preview. 8–12: show the exact accepted physical print sample; until one exists, any approved mockup must be labeled as a design preview. 12–15: the appropriate exploration or verified-product CTA.
- **Reddit copy:** “A closer look at the coffee ritual. Lupi lets you turn caffeine around in 3D; its 24 atoms make a surprisingly intricate shape. Explore the molecule.” Add a physical-product sentence only when that exact offer passes acceptance.
- **Do not imply:** Improved energy, focus, health or performance; physical availability or delivery dates before verification.

## Placement and creator handoff

Use the four registered placement pages from [the operating document](dual-launch.md#links-and-measurement). For example, `/launch/caffeine/youtube-orbit-a/` and `/launch/water/creator-cr01-a/`. The owned base page is distinct from paid and creator placements. Do not invent query parameters, claim a placement is active, or overwrite the incoming source with an internal CTA label.

- **YouTube:** Make the first seconds understandable without sound and caption the actual preview. Shorts description/comment URLs are non-clickable; use a channel-profile link, related video or actual ad CTA. Clickable long-form external links require advanced-feature access. [YouTube link guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en-UM)
- **Instagram carousel:** One observation, one molecule view, one accepted-object detail if eligible, then one clear CTA. Label mockups accurately; keep artwork pixels and product options consistent with their approvals.
- **Reddit:** Lead with the shape or ritual, explain the brand relationship and answer the scientific question accurately. Follow each community's promotion rules; avoid repeated cross-posts and unsolicited messages. [Reddit spam guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28012014962580-How-do-I-keep-spam-out-of-my-community)
- **Creators:** Supply the exact destination and compensation terms, then let the creator describe only experience they actually had. Draft paid disclosure: “Ad: Lupi paid for this video.” Draft gifted disclosure: “Lupi gave me this product for free.” Where applicable add: “I earn a commission if you buy through this link.” Put the disclosure with the endorsement, within the video in audio and visible text, and beside the link/caption; repeat it periodically in a live stream. These templates describe the actual arrangement only when it exists. [FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)

YouTube creators must also declare paid promotion in Studio for payment, gifts or other branded-content consideration; the platform label supplements a clear creator disclosure. Keep these briefs about molecular exploration and physical objects, without unsupported health claims or promotion of recreational drugs. [YouTube branded-content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17596007?hl=en)

Before scheduling, check the final creative, exact destination, disclosure, availability, US eligibility and the private loss limit. This document sends no outreach, buys no media and establishes no launch date.
