Lupi is a free native SwiftUI and RealityKit molecular sandbox. Core local play, bundled previews and the on-device collection do not require an account or a purchase.

Camera-free review:
1. From Home, choose Preview in 3D and select a bundled starter such as Water or Caffeine. Drag the model and use Recenter. The formula, atom count and source appear with the preview.
2. Return Home, choose Find a molecule, enter water or H2O, then choose Find molecule. Exact bundled names and formulas work offline. Choose Preview in 3D to inspect the result without starting AR.
3. Home > Your collection opens the local collection; it may be empty on first launch.

AR review:
Use an ARKit-compatible physical iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 26 or later. From a preview choose Play, or select a starter on Home. The camera explanation appears before the system camera-permission request. Move the device slowly until tracking is ready. Drag a molecule to hold it and flick to throw; tap it to open its plaque and choose Keep. Home > Your collection returns to kept items. LiDAR is optional; without it ARKit-detected planes provide surfaces. The Simulator cannot exercise the physical room camera. Room motion, breaking and building are illustrative play, not chemical-reaction simulation or prediction of material behavior.

Connectivity and account:
Bundled previews, exact name/formula discovery and local collection work without a network connection. Description search sends the submitted text to Lupi and Jev and returns a suggested bundled match; it does not generate the displayed molecular coordinates. No login is required to review the core app. The initial App Store release has account sign-in and cloud collection sync disabled. Trophies, rooms and shelves remain on this device. In Settings, Erase this device's collection removes local trophies, rooms and shelves.

Physical merchandise:
The bundled fallback and the currently published remote merchandise feed are empty with storefrontPublic=false, so no shop call to action is displayed. Opening a molecule preview makes an anonymous request to https://lupi.live/commerce/merchandise.v1.json; the live feed, rather than the bundled fallback alone, controls available offers. The published closed feed was verified on November 3, 2026 and must be rechecked when submitting this build. The optional commerce integration is limited to physical goods with US shipping and external Shopify checkout. A merchandise purchase never unlocks native app content or functionality. There are no paid digital features to test.
